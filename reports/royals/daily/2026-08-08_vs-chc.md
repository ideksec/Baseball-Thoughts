## Limitations

Single game; win-probability figures describe this game's swings, not underlying talent. A 4-for-4 with two homers is a genuine outlier and should not be read as a new baseline. Two data defects affect this report. First, the pack's `rolling` block reflects the game log as of generation (2026-08-10) rather than as of 2026-08-08 — it reports 49–70 through 119 games, one game ahead and with the wrong result attached; the figures above are recomputed from the committed game log using the repo's own `last_n_summary` and `season_summary`. Second, `statcast.royals_pitchers` lists opposing hitters, so no pitcher-level Statcast claims are made. Batter-level Statcast resolves names by MLBAM id and is unaffected.
